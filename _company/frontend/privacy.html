Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. What Data We Collect

During the free compliance self-assessment, we collect:

  • Company information: Industry, employee count, data storage volume, revenue (optional), PII record count (optional)
  • Assessment answers: Your Yes/No responses to 20 compliance questions
  • Email address: Only if you voluntarily subscribe for notifications

2. How Your Data Is Processed

All assessment data is processed entirely in your browser. No data is transmitted to our servers during the assessment. Your answers, scores, and results are calculated client-side using JavaScript.

If you provide your email address for notifications, it is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) until we implement a backend service.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect names, addresses, Social Security Numbers, or financial account information
  • We do not use tracking pixels from Facebook, Google, or other ad networks
  • We do not sell, share, or rent your data to third parties

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. We use browser localStorage only to save email subscriptions if you opt in.

5. Data Sources

The enforcement cases, breach costs, and compliance data displayed in results are sourced from:

  • Federal Trade Commission (FTC) public enforcement actions
  • HHS Office for Civil Rights (HIPAA enforcement)
  • State Attorney General enforcement actions
  • IBM Cost of Data Breach Report 2024

6. Your Rights

Since all data remains in your browser, you have full control. Clear your browser data at any time to remove all information.

7. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at yobizwiz@gmail.com
